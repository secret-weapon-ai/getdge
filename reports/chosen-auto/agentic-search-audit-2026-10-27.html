Selected observations

From this audit’s captured answers
1
Neither AI engine named you
Neither ChatGPT nor Google’s AI Overview named Chosen Auto Sales in any of the 50 answers captured. Those same answers named 94 other dealers and dealer groups, about 5.4 per answer.
2
Category gaps
Chosen Auto Sales wasn’t named in any of the 5 search categories tested. The busiest were Best dealer overall and Budget & down payment, tied at about 5.8 other dealers per answer, and none of them was Chosen Auto Sales.
3
What the AI cited
The AI backed its answers with 163 cited sources. 99 were other dealers’ own websites and 64 were third-party sites (other website (23), review or rating site (15), forum or social media (10)). None pointed to chosenautolv.com. Dealers’ own websites were the most-cited kind of source.
4
Map pack & page 1
In Google’s regular results, Chosen Auto Sales appeared in the top-3 map pack for 0 of 22 searches that showed a map pack and on page 1 for 0 of 25 searches. Other dealers held every map-pack spot in all 22 searches that showed a map pack.

Limitations

  • This is a sample: 25 buyer-intent queries, each run once on Oct 20, 2026 and Oct 27, 2026.
  • All 25 Google searches were captured from an out-of-state connection: Google’s page footer placed the tester outside Nevada, while the results header usually showed Las Vegas or a nearby Las Vegas-area city (a few searches showed no location there, and one showed the out-of-state location). Every query named the city, but Google’s AI Overview and especially its map pack depend partly on where the searcher is, so a shopper searching from Las Vegas may see a different map pack and page 1.
  • Searches were run logged out, with ChatGPT web search on. Signed-in users, their history, location and the time of day can all change the answers.
  • AI answers change often. The same question asked again, by someone else or on another day, may name different dealers.
  • Only ChatGPT and Google (AI Overview, map pack, page 1) were tested. Perplexity, Claude and other assistants were not.
  • This report records what each engine showed on the run date. It isn’t a guarantee of future placement in any AI answer or search result.
